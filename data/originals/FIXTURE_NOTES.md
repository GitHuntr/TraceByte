# TraceByte synthetic fixture notes

All fixtures in this directory are **synthetic** and were generated by
`src/fixtures.py` from a deterministic seed. No real-world evidence or device was
used to create them.

Fixtures beginning with a genuine file signature (JPEG, PDF, or ZIP) are **not**
valid, fully parseable files. They are controlled inputs for measuring signature
detection, extent selection, and exact byte matching. Do not describe a matching
signature alone as a valid recovered file.
